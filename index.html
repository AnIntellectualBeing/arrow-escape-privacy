1. Developer & Application Identity

This Privacy Policy outlines how An Intellectual Company ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") protects and respects your privacy when you install and play Arrow Escape (Package Name: com.anintellectualcompany.arrowescape).

Arrow Escape is an interactive puzzle mobile application designed to offer strategic grid-based gameplay. We are committed to transparency and upholding user privacy standards across all global regions including the United States, European Union (GDPR), United Kingdom (UK GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), and Brazil (LGPD).

2. Information Collection Practices

We adhere to the principle of strict data minimization. Arrow Escape operates primarily offline and does not collect personal information from its users.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or user identifiers.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not capture or record Android ID, Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, or IP addresses.
  • No Financial Data: The game does not require account registration, credit card details, or financial credentials.
  • No Location Data: We do not access, track, or record GPS location or approximate network location.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Arrow Escape requests minimal system permissions strictly necessary to deliver enhanced tactile gameplay feedback.

Requested Permission Declaration

Permission Name Purpose Network Transmission
android.permission.VIBRATE Delivers tactile haptic motor responses during arrow moves and puzzle collision triggers. No Data Transmitted

Users may enable or disable haptic feedback at any time directly within the game settings menu or via system settings.

4. Local Data Storage & Preferences

To preserve your puzzle progress across gameplay sessions, Arrow Escape uses Android's local preferences storage (Jetpack DataStore Preferences).

Data stored locally includes:

  • Unlocked level index and highest completed campaign stage.
  • In-game audio, music, and vibration preference toggles.
  • Best move count or timer high scores for solved levels.

This data resides exclusively on your physical mobile device within the application's isolated sandbox storage. It is never uploaded to remote servers or shared with external parties.

5. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

Arrow Escape does not contain third-party SDKs, behavioral ad networks, tracking cookies, or diagnostic analytics trackers.

  • No Ad Networks: No AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Facebook Audience Network integration.
  • No Analytics SDKs: No Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, or Flurry tracking.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track user behavior across other apps or websites.

6. Children's Privacy Compliance (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Protecting children's privacy is extremely important to us. Arrow Escape is suitable for general audiences and players of all ages.

We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR Article 8 regarding children's data consent):

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU).
  • We do not require account creation, social login, or personal profile setups.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided us with any personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly investigate and ensure no personal data exists.

7. User Data Rights & Data Control

Under regulations such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and CalOPPA, users possess specific data rights including the right to access, erase, and restrict processing of personal data.

Since Arrow Escape stores zero data on external servers, you hold total control over all local game data directly on your device:

  • How to Erase All Game Data: Open Android Device Settings > Apps > Arrow Escape > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the Game: Uninstalling Arrow Escape completely deletes all stored game state preferences and saved progress from your device.
